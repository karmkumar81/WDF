About StudentHub & CHARUSAT

Charotar University of Science and Technology (CHARUSAT) is committed to excellence in technology, engineering, sciences, and healthcare. StudentHub was created as a unified platform to foster collaboration, transparent communication, and student development across campus.

Our Core Objectives

  • Provide real-time academic announcements and examination notices
  • Facilitate smooth online registration for campus events, workshops, and hackathons
  • Enable transparent feedback and rapid administrative support
  • Adhere to universal web accessibility standards for all users
