Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about enrollment, campus events, and portal features.

How do I register for StudentHub as a new student?

Visit the Registration Page and provide your official university student ID, name, branch, and email address. Once submitted, your login credentials will be activated.

Can I participate in events outside of my home department?

Yes! Most university technical fests, hackathons, and workshops welcome inter-departmental participation across CSPIT, DEPSTAR, and other institutes.

How do I reset my account password?

You can request a password reset via the contact support desk or reach out to the campus IT department with your student ID verification.

Where can I view my registered events?

Log in to your account and navigate to the Student Dashboard to check enrolled workshops and confirmation status.
